Radiosurgery Dose Explorer

Real Gamma Knife treatments for vestibular schwannoma, a benign tumour on the nerve between the inner ear and the brainstem. Each image is the planning MRI with the tumour red, the cochlea cyan, the prescription dose yellow and half of it blue. The dashed pink line is the tumour as outlined by the deep learning model, which never saw these patients while it learned.

Anonymised data from the Vestibular-Schwannoma-SEG collection (CC BY 4.0). A learning project: not for clinical use.

Drag the slider or use the arrow keys to move through the head, 2 mm per step.
